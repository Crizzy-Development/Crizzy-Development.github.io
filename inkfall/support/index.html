Inkfall Support

How to reach a person, and answers to the questions testers ask.

Email crizzydevelopment@gmail.com — a real person reads it. Inkfall is in early testing, so what you noticed is exactly what we want to hear, whether it is a problem, a passage that rang false, or a turn that did not cost what you expected.

Frequently asked questions

Playing

What do I write at "What do you do?"

Whatever your character does next, in plain words. A sentence is plenty. The story picks up from what you wrote, and the world decides what it costs: there is no command list and no right answer to find.

Is the narrator an AI?

Not in the current test builds. Their passages come from text built into the app, so you are testing the worlds, the rules and the reading experience rather than the writing. Inkfall is built for a narrator written live by an AI model, and that is what later versions bring. The privacy policy says what will change when it does.

Why are some words in a passage marked?

Because the Codex has an entry for them. Tap a marked word to read what the world knows about it. The marks can be turned off under Settings, in the Reading section; the words stay tappable either way.

Your stories

Where are my stories stored?

On your device, in the app's private storage. There is no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. This version of Inkfall never uses the network, so everything works with it switched off.

Will my stories survive a new phone?

Your device's own backup carries them, Auto Backup to your Google account on Android and iCloud Backup on iOS, and restores them when you set up a new device, provided backup is switched on in your device settings and you restore the new phone from the old one with the platform's own transfer.

How do I delete a story?

From the Home screen: open the story's options and hold to delete. It is not reversible, and the app says so before you let go.

Reporting a problem

What to send

Email crizzydevelopment@gmail.com with what you were doing and what happened. Screenshots help more than descriptions. The app's version is listed in the Game State Inspector, which opens from the Debug pill on the Codex header, and it helps a lot too.
