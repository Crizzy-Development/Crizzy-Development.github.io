Inkfall Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Inkfall app for Android and iOS, published by an independent developer. Crizzy Development is the name the apps on this site are published under; it is not a separate legal entity.

Effective October 28, 2026.

The short version. Your stories, meaning the worlds you choose, what you write and everything the app records while you play, are stored on your device. They never leave it unless you send them somewhere yourself. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We collect no personal information, and nothing about you is sold or shared.

In this version of Inkfall, nothing is sent over the network at all. A later version will send your story to an AI service so that a narrator can write the next passage. This policy will be updated before that version is released, and the section on the narrator below says what will change.

Information we collect

None. Inkfall has no user accounts, no sign-in and no registration. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information, and we have no ability to identify you.

Inkfall contains no advertising software, no analytics and no third-party tracking software. In fact, significant efforts have been made to explicitly avoid such capabilities.

Information stored only on your device

The app stores the things you put into it and the things it records as you play:

  • The worlds you choose and the stories you begin.
  • What you write at each turn, and the passages the narrator returns.
  • The state of each story: what you carry, the pressures building around you, the conditions you are under, and what has come to light.
  • Which Codex entries you have seen, whether you asked not to see the intro again, and your settings.
  • A ledger of the narrator's calls: which turn, which provider and model, how much text went back and forth, and whether the result was used. Identifiers and numbers only, never story text.

All of it is held in the app's private storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us and it is not accessible to us.

Deleting a story removes its text and its state. The call ledger keeps its rows, because they describe what this installation of the app has spent rather than any one story, and they contain no story text. Uninstalling Inkfall removes all of it, subject to the backup section below.

The app can copy that ledger to your clipboard as a table, from the Game State Inspector, when you ask it to. It goes only where you paste it.

The narrator, now and later

A narrator writes the passage that follows what you do. In the version of Inkfall available today, those passages are assembled on your device from text built into the app. Nothing you write, and nothing about your story, is sent anywhere.

Inkfall is built for a narrator written live by an AI model, and a later version will bring it. That means sending what you write, together with the story so far and the state the app keeps for it, to an AI service, and receiving the next passage back. Before that version is released, this policy will be updated to name the service, to say exactly what is sent and what the service keeps, and to say how to tell which narrator you are reading. The app itself will say so too.

When Inkfall uses the network

In this version, never. The Android app declares the Internet permission because the narrator is designed to be an online service, but no part of this version connects to anything. Every feature works with the network switched off.

Backup to your own cloud account

Your device's own backup may copy the app's data to your personal cloud account, so that your stories return when you set up a new device. This is a feature of the operating system, not of Inkfall. The data goes to your account, not to us, and your platform's terms and privacy policy govern it.

  • On Android, this is Auto Backup to your Google account.
  • On iOS, this is iCloud Backup.

Either can be turned off in your device settings. Neither is controlled by Inkfall.

Crash reports

Inkfall contains no crash-reporting software.

If you have turned on your platform's own diagnostics sharing ("usage and diagnostics" on Android, "share with app developers" on iOS), your platform may make anonymous, aggregated crash and performance information available to us through Google Play or App Store Connect. That reporting is a function of the operating system and the store, is controlled by your device settings, and does not identify you to us.

Children

Inkfall is intended for adults, 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Your choices

We hold no information about you. Everything the app holds is on your device and under your control: delete a story from the Home screen (hold to delete, and it is not reversible), or uninstall the app to remove all of it. To remove a copy held by your platform's backup, use your Google or iCloud account's backup settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
