Inkfall

A narrative role-playing game that reads like a book, with a world that keeps score.

Choose a world, read the passage in front of you, and write what your character does; the story picks up from there. There is no character sheet, no dice you can see and no class to pick. What you do has consequence, paid out in story rather than numbers, and the Codex keeps what has become true. Underneath the prose a rules engine holds the state of the world, so a story stays consistent from one turn to the next and waits for you where you left it.

Inkfall is in early testing on Android and iOS and is not yet in general release. Each passage is written by an AI narrator, a generative AI model from a third-party provider, from what you wrote and the story so far; the privacy policy below says exactly what is sent to make that happen, and covers both platforms.

  • No account, and no sign-up of any kind.
  • Your stories are stored on your device. Only the narrator's requests leave it, to the AI service that writes each passage.
  • No ads and no advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking and no analytics.
