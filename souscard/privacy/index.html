SousCard Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the SousCard app for Android, published by Crizzy Development.

Effective 31 August 2026. Last updated 31 August 2026.

The short version. SousCard does not collect your information, does not share it, and has no server to send it to. Your recipes, photos and notes are stored on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

Information we collect

None. SousCard has no user accounts, no sign-in, no registration and no server operated by us. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share personal information, and we have no ability to identify you or your device.

SousCard contains no advertising software, no analytics software and no third-party tracking software of any kind.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the things you put into it: recipe titles, descriptions, ingredients, method steps, personal notes, tags, and any photos you add. This information is held in the app's private storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us and it is not accessible to us.

Deleting a recipe removes it. Uninstalling SousCard removes all of it, subject to the backup section below.

Camera, photos and documents

When you scan a recipe card or a cookbook page, capture is handled by the document scanner that is part of Google Play services on your device. When you attach a photo, it is selected through the Android system photo picker, which gives the app access to the single image you choose and nothing else. SousCard does not request permission to read your photo library.

Reading text from an image, recognising the layout of a page, and reading a PDF all happen on your device. No image, page or document is uploaded anywhere.

When SousCard uses the network

SousCard uses the internet in one situation: when you paste a recipe link into the app or share a web page to it. The app then fetches that page directly from the website you named, in order to read the recipe from it. That request goes to that website, which will see your IP address in the same way it would if you had opened the page in a browser. It does not pass through any system of ours, and we receive nothing from it.

Every other part of SousCard works with the network switched off.

Backup to your Google account

Android's built-in Auto Backup may copy the app's data to your own Google account, so that your recipe box returns when you set up a new device. This is a feature of the Android operating system, not of SousCard. The data goes to your Google account, not to us, and Google's terms and privacy policy govern it. You can turn it off in your device's Android settings, under backup.

Crash reports

SousCard contains no crash-reporting software. If you have enabled Android's own usage and diagnostics reporting, Google may make anonymous, aggregated crash and performance information available to us through Google Play. That reporting is a function of Android and Google Play, is controlled by your device settings, and does not identify you to us.

Children

SousCard is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

Your choices

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete on request. Everything the app holds is on your device and under your control: delete individual recipes in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of it. To remove a copy held by Android's backup, use your Google account's backup settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
