SousCard

A digital recipe box for people who would rather cook than scroll.

Every recipe you save becomes the same clean card: ingredients first, method below, no life story and no pop-ups. Type a recipe, clip a link, photograph a recipe book, or import a PDF — all of it happens on your device.

SousCard is on Android. An iOS version is in development; the privacy policy below already covers both.

  • No account, and no sign-up of any kind.
  • No servers of ours. Recipe scanning and text recognition run on your device.
  • No ads and no advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking and no analytics.
